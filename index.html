📖 User Guide

Welcome to the DSI Footwear Operations portal. Use the tabs at the top to navigate. Here is how to use each section:

▶️ 1. Video Viewer (Motion Analysis)

  • Search & Select: Type an operation name to find it, then click to load the data.
  • Simulate Targets: Move the Performance and Allowance sliders. The Revised SMV and Hourly Target boxes will recalculate instantly.
  • Motion Study: Use the right-side video player to slow down the footage (0.25x to 0.75x) to analyze micro-movements.

📋 2. Operation Bulletin (Sharing)

  • AI Visual Scan: Upload a photo of a shoe, and our AI will automatically scan and filter the master list below to show you the best matching operations!
  • Smart Memory: Checking boxes saves them in memory. You can clear the search bar or clear the AI filter, and your checkmarks will never disappear.
  • Preview: Hover your mouse over any image in the list to automatically zoom in and confirm the operation.
  • Generate & Share: Click Create Bulletin List to compile all checked items. Then click Copy Link (WhatsApp). Paste this link into a message. When they click it, they will see a clean, Read-Only version of your selected targets!

⚙️ 3. Manage (Admin Database)

  • Add New Operation: Enter the name, video link, and raw seconds. The system automatically converts it to SMV.
  • Save Permanently: Once you add or delete operations, click the yellow Generate Code for GitHub button. Paste that code into your master file to save it for everyone.
