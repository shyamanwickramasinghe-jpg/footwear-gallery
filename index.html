📖 FOOTTECH User Guide

Welcome to the DSI Footwear Operations portal. Use the tabs at the top to navigate the platform. Here is how to use each section:

🔑 1. Setup Your AI (API Key)

  • To use the AI Visual Scanners, you must generate a free Google Gemini API Key.
  • Go to Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com) and sign in with your Google account.
  • Click "Get API Key" -> "Create API Key".
  • Copy the new key, go to the Manage (Admin) tab in this portal, paste it, and click Save Key.

▶️ 2. Video Viewer (Motion Analysis)

  • Search & Select: Type an operation name to load existing local data. Adjust sliders to simulate target variations.
  • Motion Study: Use the right-side video player to slow down footage (0.25x to 0.75x) to analyze micro-movements accurately.

🌐 3. AI SMV Estimator

  • Global Benchmark Breakdown: Select upper and sole materials, upload a photo or video of a new style/process, and generate a comprehensive SMV breakdown for Cutting, Sewing, and Assembly.
  • Cost Ratios & Print: View the estimated Labor vs. Material ratio and click Print Costing Sheet to export an executive summary.

📋 4. Social Media OP Bulletin (Sharing & Matching)

  • 📸 AI Visual Scan: Take a photo of a shoe sample from your phone and upload it here to find matching operations from our local database.
  • 🌐 Web Match: Upload a photo of unknown machinery. The AI will generate direct YouTube search links to find global tutorials.
  • Export & Print: Check the boxes of the operations you need. Download a CSV, print a PDF, or copy a WhatsApp link.

⚙️ 5. Manage (Admin Database)

  • ⚠️ TEAM MEMBERS SHOULD NOT USE THIS TAB. This section is exclusively for Industrial Engineering Admins managing the master database.
  • Add New Operation: Enter the data. Use the Vector Builder to click points to draw lines, and drag the blue dots to manually curve shapes.
  • Save Permanently: Click Generate Code for GitHub and paste that code into your master file.
