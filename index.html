📖 FOOTTECH User Guide

Welcome to the DSI Footwear Operations portal. Use the tabs at the top to navigate the platform. Here is how to use each section:

▶️ 1. Video Viewer (Motion Analysis)

  • Search & Select: Type an operation name to find it, then click to load the video and data.
  • Simulate Targets: Move the Performance and Allowance sliders. The Revised SMV and Hourly Target boxes will recalculate instantly.
  • Motion Study: Use the right-side video player to slow down the footage (0.25x to 0.75x) to analyze micro-movements accurately.

📋 2. Operation Bulletin (Sharing & AI)

  • 📸 AI Visual Scan: Take a photo of a shoe sample from your phone and upload it here. The AI will automatically visually scan the photo and find the matching operations from our local database.
  • 🌐 Web & YouTube Match: Upload a photo of unknown machinery or a new operation process. The AI will analyze it and generate direct YouTube search links to find global tutorials and demonstrations.
  • Export & Print: Check the boxes of the operations you need. You can then download a lightweight CSV file for software importing, print a clean PDF, or copy a direct link to share the list via WhatsApp.

⚙️ 3. Manage (Admin Database)

  • ⚠️ TEAM MEMBERS SHOULD NOT USE THIS TAB. This section is exclusively for Industrial Engineering Admins managing the master database.
  • Add New Operation: Enter the name, video link, and raw seconds. Use the Snap-to-Grid Builder to click points and draw perfect geometric blueprint patterns.
  • Save Permanently: Click the yellow Generate Code for GitHub button and paste that code into your master file to save changes for the entire team.
