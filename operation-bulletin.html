📖 Operation Bulletin — User Guide

📸 1. Adding & Viewing Sample Photos

  • Drag & Drop: Drag up to 6 image files directly into the photo box.
  • Clipboard Paste: Copy any image or screenshot and press Ctrl + V anywhere on the page.
  • Preview Picture: Hover your mouse over any thumbnail to instantly view a larger popup preview.

📥 2. Excel & AI Image Import

  • Smart Excel Import: Use the "Standard Entry" tab and drop Excel sheets into the purple zone. It detects columns automatically.
  • AI Image Scanner: Click the 🤖 AI Image Scanner tab. Drop an image (or press Ctrl+V) of a simple table into the pink zone, and the AI will extract the text for you!

🏷️ 3. Bulk Work Center & Part Tagging

  • Select multiple operations using the checkboxes on the left of the table.
  • Type or choose a Work Center (e.g., Cutting) or Component Part (e.g., CU) in the toolbar and click Apply.
  • The Part-Wise SMV Summary table below updates automatically.

☁️ 4. Cloud Database & Two-Way Search

  • Save to Cloud: Enter an Item Number and click 💾 Save to Cloud to store it in the Google Sheet.
  • Two-Way Search: Inside 🔍 Load DB, use the left bar to filter by style/process/notes, and the right bar to find specific operations.
