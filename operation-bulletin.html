📖 Operation Bulletin System — User Guide

🏢 Phase 1: Central IE Team (Task Assignment)

  1. Enter Core Details: Type in the Item Type / Sample Number (this acts as the unique ID for the task). Select the Process Name, Target Plant, and the specific Allocated IE Member from the dropdowns.
  2. Add Video Links & Comments: Paste the OneDrive/SharePoint video link into the 🔗 Cloud Video Link box. Add any specific guidelines into the General Comments box.
  3. Upload Media: Drag and drop up to 8 photos and 1 Details Sheet (Excel/PDF) into the Media & Documents box.
  4. Auto-Save & Assign: Click the pink 📧 Email Allocated IE button at the top of the screen. The system will automatically save the media and text to the cloud, then open a pre-written email to the assigned Plant IE. Hit "Send" on the email.
⚠️ Important Note on Videos: Do not drag and drop raw video files into the media upload box, as this will crash the Google Sheets database limit. Always upload videos to DSI OneDrive/SharePoint first and paste the "Share Link" into the designated Cloud Video Link box.

🏭 Phase 2: Plant IE Team (Calculation & Submission)

  1. Open the Assignment: Open the email from the Central IE team. Note the "Item / Sample Number" and click the provided System Link at the bottom of the email.
  2. Load the Task: Once the web page opens, click the orange 📥 Load Assigned Task button at the top of the screen. Type in the Sample Number from your email. The system will instantly download the photos, video links, details sheet, and comments from the cloud.
  3. Review the Media: Hover over the photos to zoom in. Click the "Details Sheet" icon to download the attached document, and click the Video Link to watch the operation.
  4. Draft the Bulletin in Excel: Open Microsoft Excel on your computer. Create your Operation Breakdown (ensure you have columns for Operation Name, SMV, Machine Type, Part) and calculate your values.
  5. Upload the Completed Breakdown: In the web system, ensure you are on the ⌨️ Standard Entry & Excel tab. Drag and drop your completed Excel file into the purple "📥 Drag & Drop Excel" box. The system will automatically read your Excel file and populate the table.
  6. Save to Cloud: Review the rows to ensure the SMVs and Totals are correct, then click the blue 💾 Save to Cloud button at the top to permanently save the completed bulletin to the central database.
  7. Confirm Completion: Go back to your email client and reply to the original assignment email from the Central IE team, mentioning: "Done. The completed bulletin has been saved to the cloud."
